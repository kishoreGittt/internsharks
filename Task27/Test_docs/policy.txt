Company Remote Work Policy

Our company supports remote work to provide employees with flexibility while maintaining productivity and communication.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with approval from their manager. Employees must remain available during their assigned working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection and a suitable workspace. Company data must be handled securely, and employees should use approved company devices and applications.

Employees must inform their manager in advance if they are unable to work remotely on a scheduled day. Any changes to the remote work schedule should be communicated through the company's official communication system.

Managers are responsible for ensuring that remote work arrangements do not affect team productivity or project deadlines.

Project Nova budget is $50,000.
The company reviews its remote work policy periodically and may update the policy based on business requirements.
